SocialTech Technology Services

Tech privacy notice

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This notice covers information collected through the SocialTech Technology Services website at socialtech.au/tech and its enquiry form. It does not describe how customer device fleets are managed under a separate service agreement.

P17: Draft based on the Tech site's implementation and OAIC guidance. Before enabling collection, SocialTech must confirm the responsible legal entity, actual enquiry retention practices, hosting and email providers, likely overseas disclosures and their countries, and approve this notice. The OAIC link is an explanatory reference, not SocialTech's policy or a certification of compliance.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we collect the name, work email, organisation and service needs you provide. You may also choose to share your phone number, sector, fleet size, device types, current platform, timeframe and a message. Please do not include passwords, sensitive personal information or information about other people that we do not need.

Our hosting services may record technical information such as IP address, browser details, requested pages and access times for operation and security. When enabled, the form uses hashed request identifiers to limit spam and duplicate submissions.

Why we collect it

We use enquiry information to respond, assess your requirements, arrange a technical discussion and prepare recommendations or a quote. We do not use this form to subscribe you to marketing messages or sell your contact details.

Providing information is voluntary. Without enough contact information, we may be unable to reply or assess your request. You can make a general enquiry using a pseudonym where practicable, including by emailing contact@socialtech.au.

How we handle and share it

Enquiries are sent to contact@socialtech.au for our technical team to respond. Website and email providers process information to operate those services. We may also share information with a specialist who needs it to respond to your request, with your authorisation, or where required or authorised by law.

Hosting and email services may process information outside Australia. The locations used for the Tech enquiry service are being confirmed before online submissions are enabled. Contact us for the current arrangements.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. The website uses HTTPS. No internet or email service can guarantee absolute security.

We retain enquiry information while it is needed to respond, provide requested services or meet applicable legal obligations. When it is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to those obligations.

Website preferences

The Tech site uses browser session storage to remember review mode and, where supplied in the URL, campaign referral tags during your visit. Its current release does not load advertising, analytics or session-recording services. External websites have their own privacy practices.

Access, correction and complaints

To request access to or correction of your information, or to raise a privacy concern, email contact@socialtech.au with the subject “Privacy enquiry”. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will consider your request, investigate any complaint and explain our response and the available next steps.

If you remain dissatisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. Its website explains which complaints it can handle and how to raise them.

About the Privacy Act

For official information about Australia's privacy framework, see the OAIC's guide to the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

We will update this notice when the Tech site's information handling changes. You can request a copy by email.
