SocialTech Technology Services

Shadow AI privacy notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This notice covers the SocialTech Technology Services pages at socialtech.au/tech and socialtech.au/shadow-ai, including their enquiry forms. It does not describe how customer device fleets are managed under a separate service agreement. We update this notice when our information handling changes.

Information we collect

When you contact SocialTech Technology Services, we collect the name, email, organisation and service needs you provide. You may also share your phone number, sector, fleet size, devices, current platform, timeframe and message. Please do not include passwords, sensitive personal information or unnecessary information about other people.

Providing information is voluntary. Without enough contact information, we may be unable to reply. You can make a general enquiry using a pseudonym where practicable, including by email.

Hosting services may record IP addresses, browser details, requested pages and access times for operation and security. Our forms use hashed request identifiers to reduce spam and duplicate submissions. This anti-spam state does not store enquiry contents.

How we use and share it

Enquiries go to contact@socialtech.au so our technical team can respond, assess your requirements, arrange a discussion and prepare recommendations or a quote. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing messages. We do not sell your contact details.

Website and email providers process information to operate these services and may process it outside Australia. We may also disclose information with your authorisation, to a specialist who needs it to respond to your request, or where required or authorised by law. You can ask Felipe about the arrangements relevant to your enquiry.

Security and retention

We use HTTPS and take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No internet or email service can guarantee absolute security.

We retain enquiry information while it is needed to respond, provide requested services or meet applicable legal obligations. When it is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to those obligations.

Website preferences and assessment

These pages use browser storage for interface preferences and, on the Tech page, campaign referral tags during your visit. They do not currently load advertising, analytics or session-recording services. External websites have their own privacy practices.

The Shadow AI assessment runs in your browser and does not access your systems or company data. Its answers and result are sent to us only if you choose to attach them to an enquiry. It is an indicative self-assessment, not an automated decision about a person.

Access, correction and complaints

For privacy enquiries, access or correction requests, or a complaint, contact Felipe at felipe@socialtech.au. You can also reach SocialTech at contact@socialtech.au or 0410 390 669. SocialTech Technology Services is based in Perth, Western Australia.

Please describe your request and how we can reply. We may need to verify your identity. We will consider your request, investigate any complaint and explain our response and available next steps. You can request a copy of this notice by email.

If you remain dissatisfied, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner explains the complaints it can handle and how to raise them. For official information about Australia's privacy framework, see its guide to the Privacy Act 1988.
