Shadow AI privacy notice

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This notice covers socialtech.au/shadow-ai and its enquiry form. It does not describe how customer device fleets are managed under a separate service agreement.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry, we collect your name, work email, organisation and service needs. You may also provide a phone number, sector, fleet size, current platform, timeframe and message. Please do not include passwords, sensitive personal information or unnecessary information about other people.

The six-question assessment runs in your browser and does not access your systems or company data. Its result is sent to us only if you choose to attach it when submitting an enquiry.

How we use and handle it

Enquiries are sent to contact@socialtech.au so our technical team can respond, assess requirements, arrange a discussion and prepare recommendations or a quote. This form does not subscribe you to marketing messages, and we do not sell your contact details.

Providing information is voluntary. Without sufficient contact information we may be unable to reply. You can make a general enquiry using a pseudonym where practicable, including by email.

Website hosting and email providers process information to operate these services. They may process information outside Australia. Contact us for the current hosting and email arrangements. We may also disclose information with your authorisation, to a specialist who needs it to respond to your request, or where required or authorised by law.

Security and retention

The website uses HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information, although no internet or email service can guarantee absolute security. The form uses limited request identifiers to reduce spam and duplicate submissions; its anti-spam state does not store enquiry contents. Hosting services may record technical access information for operation and security.

We retain enquiry information while it is needed to respond, provide requested services or meet applicable legal obligations. When it is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to those obligations.

Access, correction and complaints

To request access or correction, ask about our information handling, or raise a privacy concern, email contact@socialtech.au with the subject “Privacy enquiry”. We may need to verify your identity. We will consider your request, investigate a complaint and explain our response and available next steps.

If you remain dissatisfied, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner explains the complaints it can handle. For Australia's privacy framework, see its guide to the Privacy Act 1988.
